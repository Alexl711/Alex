YourPace Business Administration

Discover our 100% online Business Administration Degrees

Our Bachelor of Arts in Business Administration program, offered through UMPI’s YourPace competency-based education (CBE) platform, is designed to provide a flexible and personalized pathway to earning your degree. This program, with its robust core curriculum and multiple concentrations, empowers you to tailor your studies to align with your career goals. You’re in control, with the ability to choose from concentrations such as Accounting, Leadership, Marketing, Project Management, and Supply Chain Management.

The curriculum is carefully developed to ensure comprehensive exposure to essential business concepts, including accounting, finance, communication (written and oral), organizational behavior, marketing, change management, human resource management, project management, and entrepreneurship. Students not only learn these key topics but also apply them in real-world scenarios, demonstrating mastery of key topics and enabling them to progress efficiently through the program.

Students build critical business management and organizational skills through self-paced, online learning on the innovative YourPace platform. This platform, complemented by hands-on assessments and practical applications, empowers learners to adapt and thrive in today’s dynamic global marketplace. It offers interactive tools, personalized support, and a robust learning community, preparing graduates to lead with innovation, manage change effectively, and excel in diverse professional roles.

With an emphasis on flexibility, affordability, and proficiency, the YourPace Business Administration program equips students to succeed in an ever-evolving professional landscape. We understand the importance of your investment, and our program is designed to provide a high-quality education at a reasonable cost.

What Is Competency-Based Education?

Designed to leverage your previous knowledge, college coursework, and work experience, competency-based education enables you to earn your degree at your pace as you demonstrate mastery of relevant academic content.

How our YourPace online programs save you time and money:

  • Prove your knowledge, move ahead—the faster you advance, the more you save
  • Flat tuition rate per 8-week session, no matter how many courses you complete
  • With no weekly deadlines, you control the speed of completion*

*Time to completion varies by student and is based on several factors including prior college coursework, relevant work experience, amount of time dedicated to studying, and the number of courses completed each session.

YourPace Business Administration Program Offerings

In addition to a traditional classroom setting, adult learners seeking a BA in Business Administration or MAOL Masters’s degree have the option of participating in UMPI’s YourPace program, our online competency-based degree completion program (CBE).  By its nature, the YourPace program offers adult learners an adaptive platform to move through competencies with content focused on mastery of stated outcomes. The competencies are structured to recognize your previous learning, allowing you to spend more time on the information you are less familiar with, saving you time to completion. We have mapped these competencies back to our traditional courses that in turn, lead to a bachelor’s degree in business administration.

Discover our 100% online B.B.A. in Accounting

Our online Bachelor of Arts in Business Administration – Accounting Concentration online program is designed to prepare you for in-demand financial and managerial roles that require an accounting background. Develop your skills in applying the principles and tools of accounting and finance to solve real-world problems, integrate knowledge of business intelligence into your career, and increase your earning potential.

In this accredited online business administration accounting program, you will learn practical applications of financial planning expertise that is essential to ensuring an organization has a budget to meet its needs and achieve its goals. You will be exposed to all major business functions as you learn to adapt to rapid changes in a technologically dynamic global marketplace. Grow your professional communication, critical-thinking, and decision-making abilities. Experiential learning activities provide opportunities to devise strategic solutions to situations facing a hypothetical organization.

In this B.A. in Business Administration – Accounting online program, you will learn to:

Frequently Asked YourPace Business Administration Questions

The YourPace Business Administration program is an online, competency-based education (CBE) degree designed for learners who want to complete their degree at their own pace. This program allows students to demonstrate mastery of specific skills and knowledge areas to progress quickly through the coursework.

This program is ideal for adult learners, working professionals, or anyone with prior college credits or relevant work experience looking to complete their degree affordably and flexibly.

Students can tailor their degree by choosing from the following concentrations:

  • Accounting
  • Leadership
  • Marketing
  • Project Management
  • Supply Chain Management

In a competency-based model, you progress by demonstrating mastery of specific skills or topics rather than following a fixed schedule. This approach lets you focus on areas where you need development while bypassing content you already know.
