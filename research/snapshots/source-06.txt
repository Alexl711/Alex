YourPace Tuition & Fees
At the University of Maine at Presque Isle, we are committed to helping our motivated students access the resources needed to reach educational goals and make dreams a reality. That’s why we offer the same affordable, flat tuition rate no matter how many courses you complete each session—the more courses you complete, the more you save. Apply what you know to move through courses self-paced, and earn a bachelor’s degree for $10,200* or less.
Explore our tuition rates, payment options, and refund policies to help you manage your finances effectively.
*Time to completion varies by student and is based on several factors including prior college coursework, relevant work experience, amount of time dedicated to studying, and the number of courses completed each session.

Financial Aid Opportunities
As a YourPace student, you may be eligible to receive federal financial aid. We encourage you to apply for financial aid as soon as possible to prevent processing delays. To apply for financial aid, complete the Free Application for Federal Student Aid (FAFSA) at fafsa.ed.gov. UMPI’s Federal School Code is 002033. Please note that U.S. Federal Financial Aid is not available to international students.

Military Tuition Assistance (TA)
The University of Maine at Presque Isle (UMPI) participates in a partnership with the U.S. Department of Defense. As part of this partnership, the university has an active Memorandum of Understanding with the DoD allowing us to maintain our commitment to supporting the education of our military-affiliated students using tuition assistance.
You must be currently serving in an active or reserve capacity to be eligible to receive Tuition Assistance.

Meet Our Financial Services Team
Our dedicated financial services staff are here to assist you with any inquiries you may have. Housed within our office is the Bursar (think Business Office) as well as Financial Aid and Student Employment staff. We work very closely with the Office of Student Records–conveniently located directly across the hall–to provide you with a dedicated student services entity. Our top priority is to help students get as much of their University “business” done in one spot as we can–and to do it as quickly and efficiently as possible.
