    Institutional Catalog

    The WGU Institutional Catalog contains detailed descriptions of WGU's colleges and degree programs, including information on accreditation, regulatory compliance, admission requirements, courses associated with each degree program, and course descriptions. It also details university support systems, governance, faculty, and other academic and administrative topics. Information found in the catalog can also be found on various pages throughout the WGU website.
