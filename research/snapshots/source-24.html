Overview of UMPI’s Credit for Prior Learning Program

The Credit for Prior Learning (CPL) program at UMPI allows students to earn college credit for previous knowledge, training, and experience. UMPI’s affordable CPL fees ensure accessible options for diverse learning pathways.
