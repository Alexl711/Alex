Affordable Credit for Prior Learning Fees at UMPI
Discover the fee schedule for Credit for Prior Learning (CPL) at the University of Maine at Presque Isle (UMPI) to help you advance your academic journey with minimal costs.
Overview of UMPI’s Credit for Prior Learning Program
The Credit for Prior Learning (CPL) program at UMPI allows students to earn college credit for previous knowledge, training, and experience. UMPI’s affordable CPL fees ensure accessible options for diverse learning pathways.
Fee Schedule for Credit by Examination
Learn more about the fees associated with various exam options to earn CPL credit:
